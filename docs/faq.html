Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions​

What is Replit AI?​

Replit AI is a tool that allows you to create and edit applications without writing code, using natural language instructions instead.

Do I need programming experience to use Replit AI?​

No, Replit AI is designed for non-technical users. You can create and modify applications by describing what you want in plain language.

Is Replit AI free to use?​

Replit offers both free and paid plans. The free plan provides access to basic features, while paid plans offer additional capabilities, resources, and support options.

Technical Questions​

What types of applications can I build with Replit AI?​

You can build a wide variety of web applications, including but not limited to personal websites, blogs, dashboards, simple games, calculators, and productivity tools.

Can I deploy my application to a custom domain?​

Yes, with paid plans you can connect your application to a custom domain. Free users can access their applications through the Replit subdomain.

How do I save my work?​

Your work is automatically saved as you make changes. You can also create versions of your application to mark significant milestones.

Troubleshooting​

My application isn't working as expected. What should I do?​

First, try refreshing the page. If that doesn't work, check the troubleshooting section in the documentation. You can also ask for help in the Replit community forums.

Why is my application loading slowly?​

Application performance can be affected by several factors, including the complexity of your application, the resources allocated to your repl, and current server load. Upgrading to a paid plan may improve performance.

Can I recover a deleted application?​

Recently deleted applications can sometimes be recovered from the trash section. Contact support if you need help recovering an important application that was deleted.

Account Questions​

How do I change my password?​

You can change your password in the account settings section of your profile.

Can I collaborate with others on my application?​

Yes, Replit supports real-time collaboration. You can invite others to view or edit your project using the share button.

How do I get help if my question isn't answered here?​

You can reach out to the Replit support team through the help center or post your question in the community forums for assistance from other users.

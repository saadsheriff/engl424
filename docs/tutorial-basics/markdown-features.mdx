# Troubleshooting & Best Practices

Learn how to overcome common challenges and optimize your experience with Replit AI.

## Common Issues

:::tip Solution Focused
Always describe what you're trying to achieve rather than focusing on technical details.
:::

### Unclear Results

If Replit AI doesn't generate what you expected:

1. **Be more specific** in your description
2. **Provide examples** of what you're looking for
3. **Break down complex requests** into smaller steps
4. **Use iterative refinement** rather than trying to get everything perfect in one go

### Performance Issues

If your application runs slowly:

1. **Simplify complex features** where possible
2. **Optimize data structures** by explaining your needs clearly
3. **Consider splitting** very large applications into multiple smaller ones

### Effective Descriptions

The quality of your description directly impacts your results:

- <Highlight color="#25c2a0">Good</Highlight>: "Create a to-do app where users can add, complete, and delete tasks"
- <Highlight color="#DC143C">Less Effective</Highlight>: "Make a task app"

### Iteration Is Key

Most successful applications involve multiple rounds of refinement:

1. **Start with a basic version**
2. **Test and identify improvements**
3. **Request specific enhancements**
4. **Repeat until satisfied**

## Advanced Tips

:::danger Avoid Technical Jargon

Using technical terms may confuse the AI. Focus on describing functionality in plain language.
:::

### Templates and Reuse

- **Save successful prompts** for future use
- **Create template applications** as starting points
- **Document what works well** for your specific needs

### Collaborative Development

- **Share precise instructions** when working with others
- **Maintain consistent terminology** across team members
- **Document application capabilities** for users
